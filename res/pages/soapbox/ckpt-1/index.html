I really wanna do the commissions. I NEED to do the commissions—I’ve had them for almost two years now. But they feel so daunting now. The longer I don’t do them, the more I feel I have to put more effort into making sure my clients get their money’s worth. And then it feels even more daunting. It doesn’t help that I feel exponentially more like a failure with each passing day. Doing art should never be a thing that causes me stress, but, since it does, I have a psychological aversion to it.

Ugh.

It doesn’t help that I’m so distractible and that I keep a list of ever-expanding to-dos. I start on one thing, get interested in another, start working on that, think of cool things to add, then start working on one of those features or whatever. That’s how I’ve ended up with this dumb website.
