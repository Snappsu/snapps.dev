Now, I'm writing this post because I'm in a better place now! My penultimate semester is done, and I can finally forfeit the reins and let fate take its course.

There's a sort of tranquility that comes with it. All that pent-up anxiety, built from hours and hours of laboring away at projects and working betwixt dense branches of arbitrary constraints, just gone.

I did all I could.
I may not have been perfect.
I may have tripped a few times.
I may have missed a few spots.
But I don't have to worry about it anymore!

I was cutting it real close this semester, but I should be fine.

=== TANGENT: (SCHOOL)WORK ETHIC ===

With every semester of school, I've become increasingly more jaded and less interested in actually learning.

Though given that most of my graduate classes are literally the same class as the undergrad one, but with a research paper tacked on, I guess I'm not missing out on too much.

:snappsSigh:

I used to give it 120% all the time back in undergrad, but now that I'm nearing the end of my time in school, I find myself optimizing for minimal effort: I do exactly as well as I need to and then just give up on even trying to learn anything more. Heck, I went, and fuggin skipped the final project of a class this semester. Didn't even attempt to half-ass a few paragraphs. Just straight up turned nothing in. And the worst thing is, I'm gonna pass that class.

And don't get me started on my study habits (or lack thereof).

=== TANGENT END ===

I guess it doesn't really matter now: I only have one more semester, two more classes, and three reasons I'm not worried:

  1. I'll get to choose the focus of my final project.
  2. Only one of my classes will have exams I'll need to study for.
  3. I have good vibes rn, don't ruin it!

Now that I am finally liberated of all that stress, I have room to stretch out and really feel myself. I can hear all my hopes and dreams beckoning my name. I can't answer them yet, but soon.

You may have heard of the idea that a messy desk is evidence of a messy mind. Or, on the other side of the coin, you may have heard that a cluttered desk is evidence of a creative mind. Not to be a fence-sitter, but I think both can be true.

Regardless, I bring this up because somewhere between my external space and my internal thoughts, there is a mess. Right in the middle, in fact! My digital workspace is constantly the victim of both bloat and neglect. Bloat comes when I don't properly account for what I need in my workspace, and neglect comes when I get distracted from my hobbies for whatever reason. Over the years, I've become more familiar with my needs and so bloat has gone down, but school has consistently been the cause of neglect.

My digital workspace is powered by Obsidian and has been for a little over a year. I have been using my first vault—the thing that holds my docs and stuff—as my main vault for that time, and it shows. There are loose documents all over the place, dozens of plugins caked with dust, hacky scripts written out of inexperience, and then school comes around again, and it all gets forgotten about.

That's why I spent the last days of school creating a new vault! Hopefully it does me good.

I'll let you know how it goes in the next checkpoint.

Anyways, Thanks for checking in!

Until next time!
