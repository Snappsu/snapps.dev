Day 1

Beforehours

From the moment I woke up, the first day was leagues more eventful than I ever though it would be.

Let me give you the play-by-play:

I'm so excited to finally attend Furality again! Yippie!

Unity Lighting & Visual Polish in VRChat

The first full panel I got to attend was "Unity Lighting & Visual Polish in VRChat" by MyroP. I'm not planning on doing any 3D stuff soon, not for a decently long while in fact.

However, I make it a point to attend any technical/computery presentations because:

  1. I am a very curious computer scientist
  2. I plan on using this info somewhere in the future... probably
  3. I could get ideas for new projects or solutions for existing ones

But yeah, the presentation was good!

Hackers with Tails

The "Hackers with Tails" panel by Alofoxx was pretty eye-opening.

I've heard about DEFCON many times but it always seemed like a black box of sorts; I knew stuff happened inside it, but I didn't know how. In this presentation, I learned that DEFCON—and hacking culture as a whole—is a lot more open then I expected. There's a whole society of villages, each filling a specific niche. Personally, I think the village I fit best in would be the packet hacking village.

You may not know this, but GillPanda is one of the artists that inspired me to start drawing. Namely, it was this piece (nsfw) that really incited my passion to draw (any my love of kobolds).

Gosh, I'm crying all over again as I type this. :snappsSobbing:

It was all coming to an end.

No amount of tears was gonna change that, but I let them out anyways.

And just like that, it was over.

A deep silence washed over the platform.

One by one, distant goodbyes and acquiescent farewells reverberated though the instance.

Talks of meeting up in Discord danced in the air.

Invites and portals flew everywhere.

And there I was.

Crying all by my lonesome.

Not tears of pain or isolation, mind you, but tears of... realization.

I didn't understand that at first, but I do now, as I'm typing this.

I realized that I have found my passion; I wanna make stuff that makes people smile, if even for a moment.

I realized that I have found my home; the warm, fuzzy embrace of the furry community, if they will have me.

I realized that I have found my next chapter in life; one where I take each step with confidence, if fate deems it so.

Of course, it's up to me to take that first step.

But that's enough waxing poetic for like two or three checkpoints.

Reflection

Since this is my first post-event post, I wanna establish a section for reflection.

To begin:

It's amazing how Furaity works behind the scenes. It's all volunteer work, if you didn't know! It makes hosting a little community seem feasible to me.

Another thing I realized is (that I vastly underestimate) how many furries there are out there. I can't help but wonder how man have intentionally left their comfort zone and interacted meaningfully with the wider furry community.

On that note—and I acknowledge this is not a novel concept—the label 'furry' has a lot of possible meanings, and I think those meanings can be attributed to either being in the 'community' or being in the 'fandom'.

Do note that, according to my philosophy, you must be a part of the fandom to be a part of the community. I think makes sense. (Also this is a non-exaustive graph, ofc.)

While I have labeled myself as a furry—well, scalie—for about six years now, I've only somewhate recently been actively diving into the community.

I didn't have this confidence at first, but between attending Furality and playing with my TTRPG group, I feel so much more prepared to talk to random people.

Though I'm sure it won't be all sunshine and rainbows. :snappsSquint:

All that said, I really wanna start going to more (VR) furry conventions! Even more than that, I want to contribute to such conventions!

Hopefully I don't sound too melodramatic, but like—and I can not stress enough how sincerely I mean this—the furry community feels like a second home to me.

If you've made it to here, you have my deepest appreciation. I know this was a hefty bunch of bytes to read through, so thank you.

My next post should be shorter.
